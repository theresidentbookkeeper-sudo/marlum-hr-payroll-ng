V16 adds attendance and time management for casual staff, approval of attendance records, duplicate-date controls, payable-day calculations and direct linkage of approved attendance to weekly casual payroll.

Employee Master Data

If the employee list you see doesn't match the latest master list a developer has built into the app, it's because your browser is still using data saved from an earlier session — this app remembers what you last had, on purpose, so your work isn't lost between visits. Use this if you specifically need to discard that and start from the current build's employee list instead.

This replaces the employee list only. Payroll cycles, attendance, HR records and everything else are kept exactly as they are.

Rename or Merge a Site / Department

Moves every employee currently in one department to another — use this to fix a typo, merge two names that turned out to be the same physical site, or give a department a clearer name. This updates every affected employee's record; it does not touch attendance, payroll, or loan history, which are tied to the employee, not the department name.

Company Salary Payment Account

The account salary payments are made from. Shown for reference on generated bank upload files — this does not move money by itself.

Direct Bank Payment Integration Not Connected

This lets the app initiate salary payments automatically instead of exporting a file for manual upload. It requires a corporate API integration with your bank and is not active yet — the fields below are ready to be filled in once that integration exists, but no payment will be sent until they are.

See the "What's needed for Direct Payment" note on the Payroll Cycle page for exactly what to request from your bank before this can go live.
